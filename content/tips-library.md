# Bank your cash, then conquer Leonida

GTA 6 punishes careless habits much harder than GTA V did, and this matters more to a beginner than any button layout. **Cash you carry is gone when you die or get arrested**, saves happen only between missions, police remember your outfit, face, car and gun, and the big money stays locked until Chapter 3, so early grinding mostly wastes time. Grand Theft Auto VI launched on **19 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only**. There is no PC version and no online mode, so every tip below is for console story mode ([Rockstar](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI); [GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/articles/gta-6/when-is-gta-6-online-coming-out)). Advice from launch-week guides and creators keeps coming back to the same habits: bank money at ATMs after every job, save manually into a fresh slot, put a suppressor on your gun early, scan cars for trackers before you steal them, and keep a spare outfit in your trunk. The game is two days old as of this report (21 November 2026), and most detailed numbers come from fast-moving guide sites rather than from Rockstar. Several basics are still disputed, including the character-switch button, whether Jason and Lucia share money, and what the ride app is called. The report flags those conflicts and does not pick a side. It ends with a tagged library of 119 tips ready to load into the app.

**Source quality note.** IGN, GameSpot, Kotaku, Polygon and several other major outlets blocked automated reading, so many of their claims below come from search snippets. The most fully read sources are Red Bull Games, Shacknews (review), Output Lag, GTABase, Dexerto, PowerPyx, GameRant and TechRadar's launch live blog. Rockstar has published no official controls list, patch notes or system guides. Re-check every number in-game before the app ships, because a patch can change prices, caps and unlock timings.

## Three habits protect you from the game's harshest penalties

GTA 6 is less forgiving than its predecessor. Rockstar North co-head Rob Nelson said in interviews that **undeposited cash is lost if you are killed** ([Vandal](https://vandal.elespanol.com/noticia/2026270007/asi-te-penalizara-gta-6-si-te-arrestan-o-mueres-en-combate-adios-a-tus-armas-y-a-una-buena-suma-de-dinero)). Being Busted also takes your carried cash, your stolen goods and stash bags, and any weapons or vehicles you don't own, and it may send your personal car to an impound lot that you have to break into ([GameRant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-arrested-busted-what-happens/)). Before the "Buck Me" mission opens a bank account (about one to two hours in), the only safe place for money is the **loose floorboard stash in Jason's bedroom** ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-beginners-guide)). After that, deposit at ATMs after every job ([Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/28810/13-beginner-tips-for-gta-6-i-wish-i-knew-from-the-start)).

Saving is the second trap. There are **15 manual slots**, you cannot save mid-mission, and saving is greyed out for the entire Prologue ([Output Lag](https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-beginner-tips/); [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/live/news/gta-6-launch-day-november-19)). Output Lag says there is no mission replay and recommends a new slot at the start of each chapter. Sources disagree on autosaves. Output Lag says the game autosaves only when a mission ends, while TechRadar's community roundup says it also autosaves after major open-world events, including being Wasted or arrested. Launch-week reports of lost progress, prologue crashes and an early Lucia mission softlocking after a bank scene make manual saves more important still ([GameWatcher](https://www.gamewatcher.com/gta-6-launches-to-reports-of-bugs-and-review-bias)).

The third trap is a point of no return. Starting the mission **"Tourist Trap" ends your access to Jason's house, stash and car** at the end of Chapter 1, so empty the floor stash first ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/32-spoiler-free-tips-as-you-hop-into-grand-theft-auto-6-2000758016); [Output Lag](https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-beginner-tips/)). PowerPyx goes further and calls Chapter 1 money farming pointless because the cash is lost at the end of the chapter ([PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/?p=370489)). One guide says up to $5,000 in Family Savings carries over ([GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/articles/how-to-keep-your-money-safe-in-gta-6)), but how much of Chapter 1 cash you actually lose is still unclear.

## Day-one settings: fix blur and sensitivity, because 30 fps is fixed

Every console version targets **30 fps with no performance mode**. Digital Foundry's reported testing puts PS5, PS5 Pro and Series X at about 1440p, rates PS5 Pro the best version, and finds Series S at about 720p, upscaled and often in the mid-to-high 20s ([Endgame Tools](https://endgametools.com/en/gta-6/news/gta-6-30-fps-target)). Shacknews measured 4K/30 on PS5 Pro, most likely an upscaled output ([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/151380/gta-6-review-score)). Since no graphics mode can be chosen, the most useful settings changes for a beginner are about feel. Output Lag recommends **Motion Blur at 0**, raising Look/Aim Sensitivity from 7 to **9–10**, and setting Movement Speed to Run. The game also has accessibility options: **Accessible Combat, Reduced Vehicle Damage and Simplified Mini Games** ([Output Lag](https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-beginner-tips/)). Reddit agrees on motion blur only in part, because OLED owners argue for keeping some of it. Other community suggestions are a smaller stick dead zone and proper HDR calibration (per display expert Vincent Teoh) so the image doesn't look washed out ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/live/news/gta-6-launch-day-november-19)). Inven adds keeping the minimap always on and switching units to ones you know ([Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/28810/13-beginner-tips-for-gta-6-i-wish-i-knew-from-the-start)).

GTABase says its controls list comes from the game's own control-scheme screens. On that list, **sprint is a left-stick click**, cover is R1/RB, weapons are hold L1/LB, the phone is D-pad Up, **Focus is L3+R3 together** and the map is Touch Pad/View. Three schemes are offered (Default, Classic and Left-Handed), and Classic swaps Run and Crouch ([GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/articles/gta-6/gta-6-controls-guide-ps5-xbox-button-layout)). The D-pad holds cause the most confusion. Holding Right brings up contextual partner commands such as distracting police or selling stash ([Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/28810/13-beginner-tips-for-gta-6-i-wish-i-knew-from-the-start)), and adding R1/RB switches character ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-jason-lucia-abilities-teamwork)). Sources give Hold Down three different jobs: a cinematic autopilot camera ([Inven](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/28810/13-beginner-tips-for-gta-6-i-wish-i-knew-from-the-start)), a slow-time view of nearby points of interest ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/32-spoiler-free-tips-as-you-hop-into-grand-theft-auto-6-2000758016)), and quick-selecting nearby objectives ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/live/news/gta-6-launch-day-november-19)). Hold Down most likely does different things in a car and on foot, but no source confirms that. Reports also say auto-aim lock-on is **off by default** ([GTABoom](https://www.gtaboom.com/gta-6-drops-default-lock-on-limits-guns-sprint-l3-204b)). This comes from a low-tier source, so the app should tell users to check Settings > Targeting.

## Jason and Lucia share guns and a goal, but not necessarily a wallet

The two protagonists play differently. **Jason is an ex-military heavy brawler** and **Lucia is an agile kickboxer and MMA fighter** fresh out of prison. Both can use Focus, which slows time and marks weak points, red for lethal and yellow for non-lethal ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-jason-lucia-abilities-teamwork); [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/151380/gta-6-review-score)). Beginners should know that **Lucia starts in prison and you play Jason alone for a while**, so free switching and duo robberies come later. Output Lag says the unlock is the mission "Breakfast Run", Izoate says "Coffee Break", and other sites say Chapter 2 ([Output Lag](https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-beginner-tips/); [Izoate](https://www.izoate.com/blog/how-to-switch-to-lucia-in-gta-6-unlock-her-after-coffee-break-and-fix-d-pad-confusion/)). Once it is open, switching works as the game's **cheapest fast travel**. A switch is near-instant if the two are close together, and otherwise the camera pans across the map ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-jason-lucia-abilities-teamwork)).

Each character trains separately and has their own **Criminal Profile** with five tiers from Professional to Irredeemable. Killing civilians, police or animals pushes the profile down, and **Irredeemable is permanent** for that character ([Output Lag](https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-beginner-tips/); [Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-beginners-guide)). A shared relationship meter has four tiers, from "Running on Empty" to "Hot Together". It rises through shared activities, joint robberies, small gestures and savings deposits, and falls if you ignore texts, fail joint jobs or run your partner over. Optional Chapter 1 tasks done before Lucia's release give a one-time boost and then vanish ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-jason-lucia-abilities-teamwork)). Purchased guns are shared, but Inven says **ammo, healing items and attachments are per character** ([Inven](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/28810/13-beginner-tips-for-gta-6-i-wish-i-knew-from-the-start)). Money is the biggest open question. Red Bull says the couple shares one bank account and one savings account, while other guides describe a personal checking account for each character plus a shared "Family Savings" account that can't be used for everyday spending. Until this is confirmed in-game, the app should tell beginners to deposit often in either case.

## Money arrives in chapters: dives first, cars second, vaults last

Guides broadly agree on the shape of the money curve, though the dollar figures are unverified. Inven tells players not to worry about slow money in Chapters 1–2, because **the big earning opportunities open in Chapter 3** ([Inven](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/28810/13-beginner-tips-for-gta-6-i-wish-i-knew-from-the-start)). PowerPyx lays out the ladder. Chapter 1 pays through story missions, store robberies, Gang Operations and races. In Chapter 2, Moses buys untracked stolen cars for **about $1,000+ each**. In Chapter 3, jewelry stores pay $10k–$25k, Randy's 18-car wish list pays $30k+ per supercar, and an automatic $120,000 bonus arrives at the chapter's end. From Chapter 4, large bank vaults pay **up to about $205k** for roughly five minutes of work ([PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/?p=370489)). The safest early side cash is Kenton's shipwreck dives, unlocked after "Island Dreams". They involve no police, the first wreck pays about $1,400, and dive sites show as purple chest icons. Shirley's Rum leads to a missable stash of about $1,300 ([Output Lag](https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-beginner-tips/)). GTABase found **no purchasable businesses or safehouses** in story mode and no reliable stock market. A $250,000 cheat reportedly unlocks only after the story ([GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/articles/gta-6/how-to-make-money-in-gta-6-full-money-making-guide)).

Free-roam robberies follow a repeatable loop. **Cut the alarm** (a white panel in the back room) so police are never called, **destroy the on-site footage**, and knock out the clerk instead of shooting them ([Output Lag](https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-beginner-tips/); [Respawndex](https://respawndex.com/gta-6/guides/heists)). Businesses are rated Low, Medium or High security. Safe codes are hidden on guards, notes or screens, and Focus highlights them. High-security sites upload footage to the cloud, so shoot the cameras before they see you. **Don't rob banks or loan stores until Chapter 3**: dye packs ruin the cash until the "Credit Check" mission gives you a jammer ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-beginners-guide); [Respawndex](https://respawndex.com/gta-6/guides/heists)). Bank vaults open only while an armored van is delivering cash, and robbing one triggers an instant four-star wanted level. Loot goes into stash bags that you fence at the right contact for each stage: Brian's lockup (code 7700) early, then Moses, Pay 'n' Spray, Randy and Yasmani ([Output Lag](https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-beginner-tips/); [PowerPyx](https://www.powerpyx.com/?p=370489)). Story heists are separate one-time missions with a planner, setups and crew. The only detailed breakdown comes from a single fan site, so treat its payouts as provisional ([gta6heists.com](https://www.gta6heists.com/heists)).

## Police remember your outfit, your car and the street corner

The wanted system rewards prevention more than escape. **No witness, report or alarm means no stars.** Pursuits now go up to **six stars** ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/wanted-system-explained/)). Hollow stars mean "Suspect Unknown": police know a crime happened but not who did it. Flashing stars mean they have lost sight of you, and leaving the search radius unseen clears the level ([GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/articles/gta-6/gta-6-wanted-level-explained-police-system-guide)). Icons under the stars show what police know: clothing, physical description, weapon, vehicle, a couple, or CCTV. Each has a fix. Swap to one of up to five outfits stored in your trunk, put on or take off a hat or get a haircut, holster or stash your gun (long guns must go in a trunk), respray or swap the car, split Jason and Lucia up, or head somewhere less populated if cloud CCTV caught you ([Output Lag](https://outputlag.com/guides/gta-6-beginner-tips/); [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/wikis/gta-6/how-to-lose-the-police/)). **Crime scenes stay "hot"** after you escape, so return in a different look ([GTABase](https://www.gtabase.com/articles/gta-6/gta-6-wanted-level-explained-police-system-guide)). Surrendering means immediate arrest with no extra star. Resisting successfully adds a star, and killing an officer, even by accident, harms your profile ([GameRant](https://gamerant.com/gta-6-arrested-busted-what-happens/)). Community clips show that putting your weapon away can sometimes let you **get off with a warning** ([TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/live/news/gta-6-launch-day-november-19)). No source documents a way to bribe police.

Combat is slower and more deliberate than in GTA V. The most consistent first-purchase advice across Kotaku, GameSpot, Inven and Output Lag is a **suppressor from Ammu-Nation**: quieter fights slow the police response and give you time to loot ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/32-spoiler-free-tips-as-you-hop-into-grand-theft-auto-6-2000758016); [Inven](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/28810/13-beginner-tips-for-gta-6-i-wish-i-knew-from-the-start)). Head and heart shots kill, while limb shots injure, so playing non-lethally is possible. Body armor adds a separate bar that soaks bullet damage ([Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/151380/gta-6-review-score)). Guns you pick up are lost on death, arrest or when dropped, and only owned guns come back ([GTA6map wiki](https://www.gta6map.net/wiki/gta-6/guides/gta-6-death-and-arrest)). Your car's **trunk works as a mobile armory and wardrobe**, holding stash bags, gun-store purchases and spare outfits ([GTA Boss](https://www.gtaboss.gg/gta-6/guides/gta-6-driving)). Before stealing a car, scan it with the **WAiNK phone app** to see its security, tracker level, sell value and registration cost. Advanced trackers report the theft to police, and they are expensive to remove early ([GTA Boss](https://www.gtaboss.gg/gta-6/guides/gta-6-driving); [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/32-spoiler-free-tips-as-you-hop-into-grand-theft-auto-6-2000758016)). Fuel barely matters ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-refuel-repair-cars/)). There is no map warp, so travel by RydeMe, where $100 skips the ride, by Scooter Bros car delivery, by bus or train, or by switching character ([Red Bull](https://www.redbull.com/int-en/gta-6-beginners-guide)).

## Conclusion

GTA 6's beginner curve is about **managing what you can lose** more than learning controls: cash, picked-up guns, stash bags, your Criminal Profile, an unsaved hour, or a Chapter 1 stash past the point of no return. That shapes what the app should prioritize. Onboarding cards should cover banking, manual saving and the trunk outfit before shooting and driving. The Criminal Profile and the hot-area mechanic, both borrowed from Red Dead Redemption 2, reward a quieter, stealthier style that GTA V veterans won't expect, so the app has real value to add by explaining it. Several "facts" in launch-week coverage still disagree with each other. The tip library therefore carries confidence tags, and the app should be built to update over the air, because a day-one or week-one patch could change payouts, carry caps and unlock missions. The first content refresh should cover the switch button, the money-sharing rule and the Chapter 1 cash carry-over, since every new player hits all three in their first few hours.

## App tip library

**Confidence key.** **[Confirmed]** means a Rockstar or developer statement, or at least two independent post-launch sources that agree, at least one of them read in full. **[Reported]** means one credible post-launch source read in full, or several lower-tier sources that agree. **[Unverified]** means a search snippet only, pre-release reporting, a single fan site or community post, or sources that conflict. Tips marked **Conflict** need an in-game check before shipping. Tip IDs are stable keys for the app.

### Getting Started

**GS-01 · Console only, no PC yet** [Confirmed]
GTA 6 is out on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S since 19 November 2026. A PC version hasn't been announced; an early-2027 date is only a rumor. Sources: [Rockstar][rockstar], [bitsfrombytes][bitsfrombytes]

**GS-02 · There's no GTA 6 Online yet** [Reported]
The game launched as single-player only, with no announced online date. Every money and heist tip here is for story mode. Source: [GTABase][gtab-online]

**GS-03 · Save manually at each chapter** [Confirmed]
There are 15 save slots, you can't save during a mission, and saving is blocked for the whole Prologue. Start a fresh slot at each new chapter so you can always go back. Sources: [Output Lag][ol], [TechRadar][tr]

**GS-04 · Don't trust autosave alone** [Unverified]
**Conflict:** one guide says the game autosaves only when a mission ends, while another says it also saves after big open-world moments such as being Wasted. Either way, make manual saves before risky jobs. Sources: [Output Lag][ol], [TechRadar][tr]

**GS-05 · Lost? Check the map's mission list** [Reported]
If your next story mission doesn't appear, open the Map screen and look at its mission list. Source: [TechRadar][tr]

**GS-06 · Hourglass jobs won't wait for you** [Reported]
Errands marked with an hourglass icon expire if you leave them, but main story missions don't. Do timed errands when you see them. Source: [Output Lag][ol]

**GS-07 · Mission stuck? Reload your save** [Reported]
Launch reports mention crashes in the prologue, an early Lucia mission softlock and falling through the map. If a mission breaks, load your latest manual save. Source: [GameWatcher][gamewatcher]

### Settings

**ST-01 · Expect 30 fps on every console** [Confirmed]
No console version has a 60 fps or performance mode, and PS5 Pro gives the steadiest picture. You can't change the frame rate, so tune the settings below instead. Sources: [Endgame Tools][df-endgame], [Shacknews][shack], [Streams Charts][streams]

**ST-02 · Turn motion blur down** [Reported]
Guides suggest setting Motion Blur to 0 because blur makes 30 fps look smeary. Some OLED owners prefer a little blur, so try both. Sources: [Output Lag][ol], [TechRadar][tr]

**ST-03 · Raise camera sensitivity to 9–10** [Reported]
The default look/aim sensitivity is 7, which feels slow to many players. Bumping it to 9 or 10 makes turning and aiming snappier, and setting Movement Speed to Run stops slow walking. Source: [Output Lag][ol]

**ST-04 · Keep the minimap always on** [Reported]
An always-visible minimap makes navigation and spotting police much easier. While you're in settings, switch distance and temperature units to the ones you use. Source: [Inven Global][inven]

**ST-05 · Calibrate HDR the right way** [Reported]
If the picture looks washed out, check your TV's settings so the first HDR screen (Max Brightness) calibrates properly. Source: [TechRadar][tr]

**ST-06 · Shrink the stick dead zone** [Unverified]
Some players find the default dead zone too big, which makes aiming feel sluggish. Lower it a little and test. Source: [TechRadar][tr]

**ST-07 · Use accessibility options without shame** [Reported]
Accessible Combat, Reduced Vehicle Damage and Simplified Mini Games are built in. They're a good way to enjoy the story if fights or minigames frustrate you. Source: [Output Lag][ol]

**ST-08 · Check your aim-assist setting** [Unverified]
Reports say lock-on aiming is off by default. If shooting feels hard, look under Settings for targeting or aim-assist options. Source: [GTABoom][gtaboom]

### Controls

**CT-01 · Click the left stick to sprint** [Reported]
Sprinting is L3 on PS5 and LS on Xbox, not a face button. The Classic control scheme swaps Run and Crouch if you prefer the older feel. Source: [GTABase][gtab-ctrl]

**CT-02 · Focus is both sticks clicked** [Confirmed]
Press L3+R3 (LS+RS) to slow time, highlight weak points, and reveal loot and police positions. You still have to aim yourself. Sources: [GTABase][gtab-ctrl], [Red Bull][rb-jl]

**CT-03 · Phone is D-pad Up** [Confirmed]
Tap Up to open your phone and check the time. Banking, rides, car scans and car delivery all live in apps there. Sources: [GTABase][gtab-ctrl], [Red Bull][rb-begin]

**CT-04 · Cover and weapons on the bumpers** [Reported]
R1/RB takes and leaves cover, and holding L1/LB brings up weapon selection. The map is the Touch Pad (PS5) or View button (Xbox). Source: [GTABase][gtab-ctrl]

**CT-05 · Driving basics** [Reported]
R2/RT accelerates, L2/LT brakes or reverses, Cross/A is the handbrake, and Triangle/Y gets you out. R1/RB shoots from the vehicle. Source: [GTABase][gtab-ctrl]

**CT-06 · What holding D-pad Down does** [Unverified]
**Conflict:** sources say it gives a cinematic autopilot camera, shows nearby points of interest in slow time, or quick-selects nearby objectives. It may depend on whether you're driving. Try it in both situations. Sources: [Inven Global][inven], [Kotaku][kotaku], [TechRadar][tr]

### Jason & Lucia

**JL-01 · How to switch characters** [Unverified]
**Conflict:** most guides say hold D-pad Right and press R1/RB, but some say hold D-pad Down. Check Settings > Controls on your copy. Sources: [Red Bull][rb-jl], [GTABase][gtab-ctrl]

**JL-02 · Free switching comes later** [Reported]
You play both characters briefly, then mostly Jason while Lucia is in prison. **Conflict:** guides name "Breakfast Run", "Coffee Break" or Chapter 2 as the unlock point. Sources: [Red Bull][rb-begin], [Output Lag][ol], [Izoate][izoate]

**JL-03 · Jason hits hard, Lucia moves fast** [Confirmed]
Jason is an ex-military brawler who relies on raw power. Lucia is an agile kickboxer who uses trips and grappling. Both can use Focus. Sources: [Red Bull][rb-jl], [Shacknews][shack]

**JL-04 · Switching is free fast travel** [Confirmed]
Once free switching is open, jumping to the other character is often the quickest way across the map. If they're nearby, the swap is almost instant. Sources: [Red Bull][rb-begin], [Kotaku][kotaku]

**JL-05 · Each character trains separately** [Reported]
Strength (max health) and Cardio (max stamina) each have four levels per character and fade if you stop training. Training together helps both of them. Source: [Red Bull][rb-jl]

**JL-06 · Guns shared, ammo and attachments not** [Reported]
A gun you buy is available to both characters, but ammo, healing items and attachments are reportedly bought separately for each one. Sources: [Output Lag][ol], [Inven Global][inven]

**JL-07 · Do they share money?** [Unverified]
**Conflict:** one guide says the couple shares a bank and a savings account. Others say each has personal checking plus a joint "Family Savings" pot you can't spend from. Deposit often either way. Sources: [Red Bull][rb-jl], [GTABase][gtab-safe]

**JL-08 · Keep the relationship meter high** [Reported]
The meter goes from "Running on Empty" to "Hot Together". Shared activities, joint robberies, small gifts and savings deposits raise it. Ignoring texts, failing joint jobs or running your partner over lowers it. Source: [Red Bull][rb-jl]

**JL-09 · Your Criminal Profile can lock** [Confirmed]
Each character has a five-tier profile, from Professional to Irredeemable. Killing civilians, cops or animals drags it down, and Irredeemable is permanent for that character. Sources: [Output Lag][ol], [Red Bull][rb-begin]

**JL-10 · Use your partner during jobs** [Reported]
Hold D-pad Right for context commands such as distracting cops or taking the wheel. In a chase, one drives and one shoots, and if you're shooting the AI keeps driving. Sources: [Inven Global][inven], [Red Bull][rb-jl]

**JL-11 · Play whoever carries more loot** [Reported]
In a robbery, switch to whichever character holds more bags so you control the valuable escape. A partner who sells stash for you pays slightly less. Sources: [Shacknews][shack], [Inven Global][inven]

### Map

**MP-01 · Leonida has six regions** [Confirmed]
They are Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers (swamp), Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. Sources: [Rockstar][rockstar], [GTABase map][gtab-map]

**MP-02 · You start in Key Lento** [Confirmed]
The story keeps you in this small island town for roughly the first fifth of the game. Enjoy its fishing, diving and beaches while you're there. Sources: [Shacknews][shack], [Red Bull][rb-begin]

**MP-03 · Fly to clear the fog** [Reported]
The map is hidden until you explore it. A plane is the fastest way to reveal it, and one reviewer uncovered it in about five hours of flying. Source: [Shacknews][shack]

**MP-04 · No warp fast travel** [Confirmed]
There's no "teleport" button. Use the RydeMe app, taxis, buses and trains, Scooter Bros car delivery, or a character switch. Sources: [Red Bull][rb-begin], [Inven Global][inven], [Kotaku][kotaku]

**MP-05 · Pay $100 to skip a ride** [Confirmed]
In the ride app, a $5 tip (up to twice) speeds the driver up and $100 skips the trip. **Conflict:** the app is spelled "RydeMe" in most sources and "RideMe" in one. Sources: [Red Bull][rb-begin], [Inven Global][inven]

**MP-06 · Follow strange sounds** [Reported]
Random events aren't marked on the map. Use Focus (L3+R3) to pinpoint where an odd noise is coming from. Source: [Inven Global][inven]

**MP-07 · Vice City is your mainland hub** [Reported]
After the Keys, Vice City holds the story and the fences. Yasmani and Tyrone are in Little Cuba. Driving back to the Keys takes about 10 minutes. Source: [Red Bull][rb-begin]

### Money

**MN-01 · Carried cash vanishes on death** [Confirmed]
A Rockstar North boss confirmed that undeposited cash is lost if you die, and arrest takes it too. Only banked money is safe. Sources: [Vandal (Rob Nelson)](https://vandal.elespanol.com/noticia/2026270007/asi-te-penalizara-gta-6-si-te-arrestan-o-mueres-en-combate-adios-a-tus-armas-y-a-una-buena-suma-de-dinero), [GameRant][gr-busted]

**MN-02 · Hide early cash under the floor** [Confirmed]
Until the "Buck Me" mission opens a bank account (about 1–2 hours in), stash money under the loose floorboards in Jason's bedroom. Sources: [Red Bull][rb-begin], [Output Lag][ol]

**MN-03 · Hit an ATM after every job** [Confirmed]
Once you have BuckMe, deposit your earnings straight away. Treat pocket cash as money you could lose at any moment. Sources: [Inven Global][inven], [TechRadar][tr]

**MN-04 · Shops cap pocket-cash spending** [Reported]
Stores reportedly accept at most $999 in cash, and bigger buys come from BuckMe. Keeping a few hundred dollars on you is plenty. Sources: [Output Lag][ol], [PC Gamer][pcg-bank]

**MN-05 · Empty your stash before "Tourist Trap"** [Confirmed]
This mission ends access to Jason's house, stash and car at the end of Chapter 1. Take everything out before you start it. Sources: [Kotaku][kotaku], [Output Lag][ol]

**MN-06 · Don't grind money in Chapter 1** [Reported]
Guides report that most Chapter 1 cash doesn't carry forward, and that only about $5,000 in Family Savings may survive. Focus on the story and wait for better money later. Sources: [PowerPyx][pp], [GTABase][gtab-safe], [Sportskeeda][sk]

**MN-07 · Check the mailbox outside Jason's house** [Reported]
Mission pay and fence payouts often arrive there instead of straight into your pocket. Source: [Output Lag][ol]

**MN-08 · Treasure dives are the safest early cash** [Reported]
After "Island Dreams", Kenton's shipwreck dives pay with no police risk. The first wreck is worth about $1,400, and purple chest icons mark the sites. Source: [Output Lag][ol]

**MN-09 · Don't miss Shirley's hidden stash** [Reported]
The "Get Shirley's Rum" errand leads to roughly $1,300 hidden in a wall vent near a yellow generator. It may be missable. Source: [Output Lag strangers][ol-str]

**MN-10 · The money ladder by chapter** [Reported]
In Chapter 2, sell untracked cars to Moses for about $1k each. In Chapter 3, jewelry stores pay $10k–$25k and Randy's supercars $30k+. From Chapter 4, bank vaults pay up to about $205k. Figures come from guides, not Rockstar. Source: [PowerPyx][pp]

**MN-11 · Focus reveals cash and loot** [Reported]
Click L3+R3 in shops, hideouts and robberies to highlight money and valuables you'd otherwise miss. Source: [Output Lag][ol]

**MN-12 · No businesses or stock market** [Reported]
Guides found no purchasable businesses, buyable safehouses or reliable stock market in story mode. Robberies and fences are the main income. Source: [GTABase][gtab-money]

**MN-13 · Grab the free freebies** [Reported]
Your first respray is free, Joey's Coastal Auto Care fixes the Ganado for free, and the four house repairs cost nothing and please your partner. Sources: [Output Lag][ol], [Output Lag strangers][ol-str]

### Heists & Robberies

**HR-01 · Cut the alarm first** [Confirmed]
In a store robbery, find the white alarm panel in the back room and disable it so the police never get called. Sources: [Output Lag][ol], [Respawndex][rdx]

**HR-02 · Wipe the camera footage** [Confirmed]
Destroy the on-site security recording before you leave so police can't identify you. At high-security sites, footage goes to the cloud, so shoot the cameras before they spot you. Sources: [Output Lag][ol], [Respawndex][rdx], [GTABase][gtab-wanted]

**HR-03 · Wait before robbing banks** [Confirmed]
Banks and loan stores use dye packs that ruin the cash. Hold off until Chapter 3's "Credit Check" mission gives you a jammer. Sources: [Red Bull][rb-begin], [Respawndex][rdx]

**HR-04 · Match the job to the security level** [Reported]
Targets are rated Low, Medium or High security. Take your partner on medium and high jobs so the AI handles crowd control. Source: [Respawndex][rdx]

**HR-05 · Safe codes are hidden nearby** [Reported]
Codes change each time. Look on guards, sticky notes and screens, and use Focus to make the notes stand out. Sources: [Respawndex][rdx], [Kotaku][kotaku]

**HR-06 · Vaults open only for the cash van** [Reported]
Big bank vaults can't be forced open. Wait for an armored van delivery or buy a tip from Yasmani, and expect an instant four-star wanted level. Sources: [Respawndex][rdx], [PowerPyx][pp]

**HR-07 · Loot must be fenced** [Reported]
Robbery loot goes into stash bags that you sell to a fence. Early on, drop bags in the strongbox at Brian's lockup (code 7700). Payment arrives later. Source: [Output Lag][ol]

**HR-08 · Know which fence buys what** [Reported]
Moses takes untracked cars (Ch2), Pay 'n' Spray takes tracked cars (Ch3), Randy takes supercars (Ch3), and Yasmani takes jewelry and marked bills (Ch3). Source: [PowerPyx][pp]

**HR-09 · Plan the getaway before the job** [Reported]
For loud jobs park near the exit, and for quiet ones use a nearby garage. Afterward, change clothes and car and split up. If one character gets caught, the other keeps the bags. Sources: [Respawndex][rdx], [PowerPyx][pp]

**HR-10 · Story heists happen once** [Unverified]
Big story heists use a planner and setup missions that change the final job, such as your explosives and time limit. You can't repeat them, so take the setups seriously. Source: [gta6heists.com][gta6heists]

### Combat

**CB-01 · Buy a suppressor first** [Confirmed]
It's cheap at Ammu-Nation and is the most-recommended early purchase. Quiet kills slow the police response and stop nearby enemies from being alerted. Sources: [Kotaku][kotaku], [GameSpot][gamespot], [Inven Global][inven]

**CB-02 · Where Ammu-Nation first opens** [Unverified]
**Conflict:** some guides say you can shop in Key Lento right after the tutorial, while another says it opens during the mission "Gear Up". Both put it early in Chapter 1. Sources: [Kotaku][kotaku], [Game8][game8]

**CB-03 · Wear body armor for big fights** [Confirmed]
Armor from Ammu-Nation adds an extra bar that absorbs bullets before your health does. Save it for missions and raids. Sources: [Shacknews][shack], [Bloxodes][bloxodes]

**CB-04 · Use Focus to find weak points** [Confirmed]
Focus marks lethal spots in red and non-lethal spots in yellow. Head and heart shots kill, while limb shots only injure. Sources: [Red Bull][rb-jl], [Kotaku][kotaku]

**CB-05 · Non-lethal play is a real option** [Reported]
You can wound and knock people out instead of killing them, which protects your Criminal Profile and changes how events play out. Sources: [TechRadar][tr], [Kotaku][kotaku]

**CB-06 · Picked-up guns don't stay with you** [Confirmed]
Guns taken from enemies are lost on death, on arrest, or when you drop them. Buy the weapons you want to keep. Sources: [GTA6map wiki][gta6map], [GameRant][gr-busted]

**CB-07 · Store extra guns in your trunk?** [Unverified]
**Conflict:** one guide says to keep found weapons in your car, but others say only guns you own can be stored and recovered. Test it before you rely on it. Sources: [Output Lag][ol], [GTA6map wiki][gta6map]

**CB-08 · Park close before you shop** [Reported]
Ammo, armor and explosives you buy reportedly go straight into your owned car's trunk, so bring your car to Ammu-Nation. Source: [games.gg][gamesgg-wpn]

**CB-09 · You can't carry an arsenal** [Unverified]
Carry limits are tight, reportedly two rifles and two pistols on you, with the rest in your trunk. Swapping to a sidearm is faster than reloading a rifle. Sources: [TheGamesWiki][gameswiki], [GTABoom][gtaboom]

### Wanted Level

**WL-01 · No witness, no stars** [Confirmed]
Police respond only when someone reports the crime, an alarm goes off or a tracked car reports itself. Do it unseen and nobody comes. Sources: [Dexerto][dex-w], [GTABase][gtab-wanted]

**WL-02 · Six stars are back** [Confirmed]
Your wanted level climbs with chase length and violence, up to six stars, bringing helicopters and wider searches. Sources: [Dexerto][dex-w], [Streams Charts][streams]

**WL-03 · Read the star types** [Reported]
Hollow stars mean police know a crime happened but not who did it. Flashing stars mean they've lost sight of you, so slip out of the search area. Source: [GTABase][gtab-wanted]

**WL-04 · Read the icons under the stars** [Confirmed]
Small icons show what police know: your clothes, face, gun, car, that you're a couple, or CCTV. Clear each one to cool off. Sources: [Output Lag][ol], [Dexerto][dex-l], [GTABase][gtab-wanted]

**WL-05 · Keep a spare outfit in the trunk** [Confirmed]
Your car can store up to five outfits. A quick change, even just a hat or bandana, clears the "clothing" clue. Sources: [Output Lag][ol], [Kotaku][kotaku]

**WL-06 · Change your face, not just clothes** [Reported]
Put on or take off a hat, get a haircut or wear a mask during crimes to beat the "description" clue. Sources: [Dexerto][dex-l], [GTABase][gtab-wanted]

**WL-07 · Ditch or respray the car** [Reported]
Swap vehicles or respray at a mod shop or Pay 'n' Spray to clear the "vehicle" clue. A respray only helps if that car is currently wanted. Sources: [Dexerto][dex-l], [GTA Boss][gtaboss]

**WL-08 · Holster your gun** [Confirmed]
A visible weapon is a clue, and drawn guns make bystanders call police. Long guns can't be hidden, so stash them in a trunk. Putting weapons away can even get you off with a warning. Sources: [GTABase][gtab-wanted], [TechRadar][tr]

**WL-09 · Split up the couple** [Reported]
If police are looking for a pair, send Jason and Lucia in different directions. Some guides say this drops a star, but that's unconfirmed. Sources: [Dexerto][dex-l], [GTABase][gtab-wanted]

**WL-10 · Crime scenes stay hot** [Confirmed]
After you escape, police keep watching the area. Coming back in the same look can restart the chase, so change outfit and car first. Sources: [Dexerto][dex-w], [GTABase][gtab-wanted]

**WL-11 · Hide smart** [Reported]
The map arrow tells you when you're hidden. Use alleys, bushes and unlocked doors, or hold Circle/B in a parked car to duck. K9 units track you, so keep moving. Source: [GTABase][gtab-wanted]

**WL-12 · Surrender versus resist** [Reported]
Surrendering means arrest with no extra star. Fighting back successfully adds a star, and killing an officer, even by accident, damages your profile. Source: [GameRant][gr-busted]

**WL-13 · Bribing cops isn't documented** [Unverified]
No guide describes a police bribe. Plan to escape or surrender instead. Sources: [GTABase][gtab-wanted], [Dexerto][dex-l]

### Vehicles

**VH-01 · Scan before you steal** [Confirmed]
Point the WAiNK phone app at a car to see its security, tracker level, sell value and registration cost. Sources: [Red Bull][rb-begin], [GTA Boss][gtaboss], [Output Lag][ol]

**VH-02 · Avoid cars with advanced trackers** [Reported]
These cars report the theft to police, and removing the tracker is expensive early on. Choose cars with no tracker. Sources: [Kotaku][kotaku], [GTABase][gtab-wanted]

**VH-03 · Register a car to keep it** [Reported]
Stolen cars are only yours for good once registered. **Conflict:** guides point to Pay 'n' Spray or to Moses swapping the plates. Sources: [TechRadar][tr], [GTA Boss][gtaboss]

**VH-04 · Check registration cost first** [Reported]
In Chapter 2, registering can cost many times a car's sell value, for example $1,400 to register a $125 car. Check the scan before you pay. Sources: [Output Lag][ol], [GTA Boss][gtaboss]

**VH-05 · Order your car with Scooter Bros** [Confirmed]
Far from your ride? The Scooter Bros app delivers your registered car to you, and the premium tier includes three free deliveries a day. Sources: [Red Bull][rb-begin], [Inven Global][inven], [TechRadar][tr]

**VH-06 · Your trunk is your toolbox** [Confirmed]
Car trunks hold cash, stash bags, gun-store purchases and spare outfits. Keep your own car close on big jobs. Sources: [Shacknews][shack], [GTA Boss][gtaboss], [Output Lag][ol]

**VH-07 · Fuel barely matters** [Reported]
Gas exists but rarely causes trouble. Gas stations handle light fixes, auto shops handle heavy damage, and EVs use chargers listed in the pause map's Vehicles tab. Sources: [PC Gamer][pcg-fuel], [GTA Boss][gtaboss], [Inven Global][inven]

**VH-08 · Pick the right car for the job** [Reported]
Use an SUV for durability and loot space, a truck to rip ATMs out of walls, and a fast motorbike to weave through traffic on getaways. Sources: [Inven Global][inven], [PowerPyx][pp]

**VH-09 · Grab an early plane** [Reported]
A Duster biplane sits at a small airstrip about 1.4 km from Jason's house once "Gone Fishing" is done, which makes it ideal for clearing map fog early. Source: [PC Gamer][pcg-plane]

**VH-10 · Save before raiding the military base** [Reported]
Jets and tanks at the base near the start area are tempting, but it's heavily guarded and trespassing brings a big wanted level. Make a manual save first. Sources: [Beebom][beebom-jets], [PC Gamer][pcg-hub]

**VH-11 · Garages come after Key Lento** [Reported]
You can't buy garages in the starting area. Once on the mainland, use the Vice City Real Estate app (just under a dozen garages in total). Sources: [Red Bull][rb-begin], [Output Lag][ol]

**VH-12 · Impounded? Break it out** [Reported]
After an arrest your personal car may end up in a secure impound lot, and you'll need to sneak in and drive it out. Check the My Vehicles app to find it. Sources: [GameRant][gr-busted], [GTA Boss][gtaboss]

### Side Activities

**SA-01 · Work out to raise health and stamina** [Confirmed]
Lifting raises max health and cardio raises stamina. Gains fade if you skip training and there's a daily cap, so make it a short routine. Sources: [Red Bull][rb-begin], [Output Lag][ol], [Kotaku][kotaku]

**SA-02 · Eat protein after training** [Reported]
A protein bar or shake after a workout locks in the gains. Overeating adds body fat, which exercise works off. Sources: [Red Bull][rb-begin], [Inven Global][inven]

**SA-03 · Calm hobbies build Focus permanently** [Confirmed]
Fishing, pool, golf, shooting ranges and meditation raise Focus, and unlike strength, Focus gains don't fade. Sources: [Red Bull][rb-jl], [Kotaku][kotaku]

**SA-04 · Daily buffs are cheap** [Confirmed]
Sleeping, showering, coffee, food and feeding Jason's snake Dolores give small buffs that last until you die or quit. Sources: [Red Bull][rb-begin], [GameRant][gr-daily]

**SA-05 · Some activities pay, most are for fun** [Reported]
Dives, races, Bret's hunting, crop dusting and car jobs earn cash. Pool, basketball, golf, clubs and water sports are mainly for fun and trophies. Sources: [games.gg][gamesgg-side], [Output Lag strangers][ol-str]

**SA-06 · Save before Yago's races** [Reported]
Yago's timed street race (Chapter 3, Jason only) can disappear if you miss it, so save first. Source: [Output Lag strangers][ol-str]

**SA-07 · Hunt Strangers & Freaks** [Reported]
Characters like Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete count toward the Strangers & Freaks trophy (eight strands). Random events usually give small rewards, around $200. Sources: [Output Lag strangers][ol-str], [WikiGTAVI][wikigtavi-re]

**SA-08 · Stunt jumps unlock unlimited nitro** [Unverified]
Completing all 50 stunt jumps reportedly gives unlimited nitrous, and collecting all the Vice City Flamingos cards reportedly unlocks a cheat. Source: [The Big Lead][thebiglead]

### Easter Eggs

**EE-01 · Find a literal Easter egg** [Confirmed]
Climb the tall radio tower in South Vice-Dale County (about a six-minute climb) and throw one of the colored eggs from the nest at the top. Sources: [ComicBook][comicbook], [IBTimes][ibtimes], [TechRadar][tr]

**EE-02 · Follow the dog on a jet ski** [Reported]
While swimming or jet skiing off the Keys you may meet a dog riding a jet ski. Follow it to an island full of dogs. Source: [ComicBook][comicbook]

**EE-03 · Track the Skunk Ape** [Reported]
Wait near straw-and-rope shrines in the Grassrivers forests for Leonida's Bigfoot, but watch out for its knockout gas. Talk to the hunter Kirk to turn it into a quest. Source: [ComicBook][comicbook]

**EE-04 · Visit Dexter's apartment** [Confirmed]
Trinity Apartments on Washington Island in Vice City nods to the TV show Dexter, with his tools on the roof and his boat nearby. Sources: [ComicBook][comicbook], [TheGamer][thegamer]

**EE-05 · Watch the skies after 2am** [Reported]
**Spoiler-light:** stay outdoors in Port Gellhorn after 2am and you may see a UFO abduction that leads to a strange questline. Avoid the military airfield unless you want stars. Source: [ComicBook][comicbook]

**EE-06 · Beware cats in stolen cars** [Reported]
Steal a car with a cat inside and it will attack before Jason throws it out. Source: [TechRadar][tr]

### Beginner Mistakes

**BM-01 · Walking around with a fortune** [Confirmed]
New players lose thousands by dying with cash in their pocket. Bank it the moment a job ends. Sources: [GameRant][gr-busted], [Inven Global][inven]

**BM-02 · Starting "Tourist Trap" with a full stash** [Confirmed]
Your Chapter 1 home and stash become unreachable after this mission. Empty them first. Sources: [Kotaku][kotaku], [Output Lag][ol]

**BM-03 · Going on a killing spree early** [Confirmed]
Killing civilians, cops or animals drags your Criminal Profile toward a permanent Irredeemable tier and makes police harsher. Sources: [Output Lag][ol], [GTABase][gtab-wanted]

**BM-04 · Robbing a bank too soon** [Confirmed]
Without Chapter 3's dye-pack jammer, the cash gets ruined. Stick to stores and dives until then. Sources: [Red Bull][rb-begin], [Respawndex][rdx]

**BM-05 · Stealing the shiniest car in sight** [Reported]
Fancy cars often have advanced trackers that call the police. Scan first and pick cars with no tracker. Sources: [Kotaku][kotaku], [GTA Boss][gtaboss]

**BM-06 · Relying on enemy guns** [Confirmed]
Picked-up weapons vanish when you die or get arrested. Spend early money on a gun you own plus a suppressor. Sources: [GTA6map wiki][gta6map], [Kotaku][kotaku]

**BM-07 · Searching for a fast-travel button** [Confirmed]
There isn't one. Use the ride app, Scooter Bros or a character switch instead of driving everywhere. Sources: [Red Bull][rb-begin], [Inven Global][inven]

**BM-08 · Skipping manual saves before big moments** [Reported]
With no mid-mission saves, no mission replay and launch bugs around, losing an hour of play is easy. Save before heists, base raids and timed events. Sources: [Output Lag][ol], [GameWatcher][gamewatcher]

**BM-09 · Rushing the main story** [Reported]
Optional tasks, hourglass errands and early relationship boosts can disappear as the story moves on. Do side content when it appears. Sources: [Red Bull][rb-jl], [Output Lag][ol]
