# GTA 6 Story Walkthrough

## Prologue
A short Lucia-only tutorial set in prison, with a flashback robbery. It teaches aiming, threatening, quick-time events and police searches.

### Prologue [Confirmed]
Who: Lucia
- Chat with your cellmates in Hamlet Prison (zoom with L2/LT, interact with X/A).
- Follow the guard to the counsellor's office.
- In the flashback, hold up the payday-loan shop and take the cash.
- Pass the quick-time event to shake off the bystander who grabs you.
- Run from the police and head back toward the coffee shop.
> Tip: You cannot lose the police here. The arrest near the coffee shop is scripted, so don't waste time trying to escape.
> Tip: The quick-time event is the only real way to fail.
Sources: PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-prologue-walkthrough/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/>

## Chapter 1 · Just Like Paradise
Jason settles into Key Lento: Ammu-Nation, his house and tenants, the BuckMe bank app and Lucia's release. Several errands are missable, and Tourist Trap is a major point of no return.

### Gone Fishing [Confirmed]
Who: Jason
Unlocks: Jason's house in Key Lento
- Ride with Cal in the boat and follow Donnie's plane, slowing down by each dropped package so Cal can scoop it up.
- Follow the route to the swamps and use binoculars to watch the meeting on the docks.
- Leave the boat and sneak through the Kingfisher camp to the shack at the far end, then free Donnie.
- Knock out the brute with your fists, grab the Zombix healing item and arm yourself.
- Fight or run to the seaplane, fly to the marker, land on the water and head to Brian's marina.
> Tip: Crouch and take enemies down from behind for a stealthy approach. Zombix heals you, and you can carry up to five.
! Missable: Donnie's cash sits in the white-and-red trailer on the way to the plane. Grab it before you leave.
Sources: PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-gone-fishing-walkthrough/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/gone-fishing>

### Gear Up [Confirmed]
Who: Jason
Unlocks: A long gun of your choice, Ammu-Nation
- Walk into the Ammu-Nation near Jason's house in Key Lento.
- Talk to the clerk behind the counter.
- Buy a long gun: a shotgun or a tactical rifle.
- Step outside and fight off the three Kingfisher gang members who arrive in a truck.
- Also listed as Pick Up Another Gun in some guides.
> Tip: Early on you can probably only afford the shotgun.
> Tip: Win or die, the shootout ends the mission. Dying just respawns you at home, so there is no penalty.
Sources: PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-gear-up-pick-up-another-gun-walkthrough/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/>

### Lab Fees [Confirmed]
Who: Jason
Unlocks: Brian's Lockup fence
- Go to the locked boutique, call Brian, then head inside.
- Talk to the clerk, who can't pay, and punch him once.
- Open the back-room door with code 0420 and take the acid. You can empty the register too.
- Call Brian, then drop the acid in the stash at Brian's Lockup (door code 7700).
> Tip: With no bank account yet, the $600 payment turns up later in the mailbox outside Jason's house.
Sources: PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-lab-fees-collect-brians-rent-walkthrough/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/>

### Just a Scratch [Confirmed]
Who: Jason
- Drive Jason's '70 Ganado to the marker and talk to Joey outside the garage.
- Pull into the garage and pick the free Full Engine repair.
- Choose Cosmetics, then Respray, and pick any colour.
> Tip: If you wreck the Ganado, reload a save and it will be parked outside your house again.
Sources: PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-just-a-scratch-fix-your-car-walkthrough/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/>

### Unwanted Attention [Confirmed]
Who: Jason
Unlocks: Police scanner app
- Drive with Cal to the Hamlet impound; he puts a police scanner app on your phone.
- Climb into the lot, unhook the red Phoenix from the tow truck (R3) and jump into it.
- Wait by the gate until it opens, then drive off and escape the police search area.
- Respray the Phoenix at the Hamlet Pay 'n' Spray and park it in Brian's garage.
- Pick up the $150, then swap your hat and glasses and buy a new outfit at the store next door.
> Tip: Sneak past the guards instead of knocking them out, because downed guards bring the police.
> Tip: Holding Circle/B while driving makes Jason duck, so he is harder to recognise.
Sources: PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-unwanted-attention-go-see-cal-walkthrough/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/>

### Pipework [Confirmed]
Who: Jason
- Talk to Mandy outside her house and follow her inside.
- Pick up the plunger from the bathroom floor.
- Fix the kitchen sink with the plunger (R3), then the wrench (R2/RT).
- On your way out, pick up the cigar she throws into the street.
> Tip: Skipping or interrupting Mandy's dialogue doesn't change anything.
Sources: PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-pipework-collect-mandys-rent-walkthrough/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/>

Who: Jason
Unlocks: Steal the Scooter
- When Shirley calls out to you in Key Lento, talk to her and agree to fetch her rum.
- Go to Three Sheets Liquor to the south and ask the clerk for the rum.
- Take it back to Shirley.
> Tip: Ask the clerk about the bird noises to mark an exotic-animals side activity for later.
! Missable: Shirley's errand can be missed. Do it as soon as it appears on the map.
! Missable: Gone for good once you start Tourist Trap.

### Steal the Scooter (Optional) [Confirmed]
Who: Jason
- About a day after the rum errand, Shirley calls. Agree to help.
- Swim or drive to Peggy's house behind Shirley's and take the scooter from the driveway.
- Lie to Peggy or ignore her, then ride back to Shirley's.
- Drive the scooter in through the porch door she opens.
> Tip: Swimming across the water is the quickest way to Peggy's house.
! Missable: Shirley's second errand can also be missed. Do it as soon as it appears.
! Missable: Gone for good once you start Tourist Trap.
Sources: PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-steal-the-scooter-walkthrough/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/>

### Hey Arnold! [Confirmed]
Who: Jason
- Wait for Brian's call after Pipework, then meet him at the pool bar in south Key Lento.
- Get into Brian's car and pick up Cal at the liquor store.
- Find Arnold in the search area to the south.
- Chase him by car while shooting the bikers (L1/LB to draw, R1/RB to fire), then follow him on foot to the docks.
- Choose whether to let Arnold go or not.
> Tip: L3 + R3 slows time, which makes aiming from the car easier.
> Tip: Your choice about Arnold has no lasting effect.
Sources: PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-hey-arnold-meet-brian-walkthrough/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/>

### Island Dreams [Confirmed]
Who: Jason
Unlocks: Kenton's treasure-diving questline
- Talk to Kenton at his beach shop and drive the boat out to the dive spot.
- Put on the scuba gear at the back of the boat and dive in (R1/RB to dive).
- Lead the divers through the trench to the shipwreck.
- Use the metal detector to find all three treasure chests, then free the stuck diver from the grate.
- Shoot the shark twice with the harpoon gun and lead everyone back to the boat.
> Tip: Kenton's diving jobs pause during the Tourist Trap run and come back later, so you won't lose them.
> Tip: Hold R1 near sea life to study it. This counts toward 100% completion.
Sources: GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/>; PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-full-walkthrough-all-missions/>; PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-island-dreams-work-for-kenton-walkthrough/>

### Feng Shui [Confirmed]
Who: Jason
- Go into Jason's house, grab a beer from the fridge and sit with Cal.
- Help Cal move the TV, coffee table, couch and chair.
- Play the two video games Cal puts on until they end.
- Watch the cutscene; next morning Hamlet Correctional calls about Lucia's release.
> Tip: You can't win either game, so dying fast in Street Trash III saves time.
Sources: PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-feng-shui-meet-cal-walkthrough/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/>

- Pick up groceries and drop them off at home.

### Coming Home [Confirmed]
Who: Jason
- Get the house ready and finish any errands, then wait for Lucia to call again.
- Drive to Hamlet Correctional and pick Lucia up.
- Celebrate at the Rusty Anchor bar.
- Dance with Lucia and meet Mattia.
- Deal with D-Man, then get away from the police.
> Tip: Fix up Jason's house first (bedroom wall and paint, stairs, roof via the ladder, balcony railing). Lucia notices.
> Tip: Bring her clothes from Andy so she can change, and buy two Pindayho beers at the bar to share.
> Tip: Washing your hands at a sink gives a short Focus boost.
> Tip: Cal's dating app and Donnie's offer have no consequences either way.
Sources: GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/coming-home>; PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-full-walkthrough-all-missions/>

### Clean Up After Donnie [Confirmed]
Who: Jason
- Answer Donnie's call the morning after Coming Home and go to the swamps in southwest Hamlet.
- Talk to the fisherman on the docks and inspect the package he points out.
- Take the nearby boat to the shack downriver.
- Beat the three Kingfishers and grab the cocaine from inside the shack.
- Take it to Donnie's trailer in Key Lento and put the bag in the container.
> Tip: The $600 reward turns up later in the mailbox at Jason's house.
! Missable: Reportedly, Donnie's drug drop-off stops once Swamped is finished, so sell to Donnie before then.
Sources: PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-clean-up-after-donnie-donnies-drop-walkthrough/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/>

### Buck Me [Confirmed]
Who: Jason
Unlocks: BuckMe app, Checking account with a $100 gift, Family Savings
- Go to the Fleeca bank in Key Lento and ask the teller about an account.
- Talk to the manager, who sends you to the BuckMe app instead.
- Open BuckMe on your phone.
- Move $100 from checking into Family Savings.
> Tip: Shops only take cash up to $999. Bigger purchases go through BuckMe.
> Tip: Dying costs pocket cash and some checking, so deposit cash at ATMs often (use the app's ATM Finder).
> Tip: Family Savings is locked for good, but it can never be lost. Savings goals give discounts and higher checking limits.
Sources: PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-buck-me-set-up-bank-account-walkthrough/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/>

### Down to Earth [Confirmed]
Who: Jason
Unlocks: Lucia's balcony meditation, Meditate with Lucia, First meditation spot
- Meet Brian at the marina office and follow him to Cal's boat.
- Pressure-wash all the green dirt off the boat.
- Fly the seaplane with Lucia to Bobcat Bay and fish the drug package out of the water.
- Win the shootout, then land at all three drop points.
- Hurry home and hide the drugs in the washing machine under the house, then join Lucia out front.
> Tip: Shooting enemies in the arms and legs earns professional reputation instead of violent reputation.
Sources: KeenGamer <https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/gta-6-walkthrough-all-chapters-and-story-missions-in-order/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/>; PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-down-to-earth-walkthrough/>

- On the morning after Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen.
- Follow her out to the balcony.
- Focus on her, choose Accept and meditate with her.

Who: Jason
- Open BuckMe and use the ATM Finder.

### Save for the Future [Confirmed]
Who: Jason
- Find the quest under the storybook icon in the map's quest list; it has no map marker.
- In the BuckMe app, move money from checking into Family Savings. You can do this anywhere.
- Hit the first goal of $5,000 to raise your checking limit.
- Later goals open as the story goes on and give discounts and higher checking limits.
> Tip: This savings-goal mission runs for the whole story. Reaching each goal unlocks discount perks.
> Tip: You get BuckMe Premium automatically at the start of Chapter 4, so there's no need to save $500,000 for it.
Sources: PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-buck-me-set-up-bank-account-walkthrough/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/>; PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-save-for-the-future-walkthrough/>

### Day Release [Confirmed]
Who: Both
- Order a drink at the Rusty Anchor bar, then take the speedboat to the yacht.
- Race Mattia on jet skis.
- As Lucia, shoot the champagne bottles, then find Mattia two decks down.
- Follow his sports car through Vice City at night and race to the Huxley Motel.
- Meet him at the Nine1Nine club and dance.
- Leave the apartment by jumping from the balcony into the pool.
> Tip: Losing the jet-ski race doesn't matter, and hitting every bottle only changes a line of dialogue.
> Tip: Slow-motion aim is L3+R3. If you wreck the car while following, reset it for a full repair.
> Tip: Riding together and holding hands can raise the relationship.
Sources: PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-day-release-meet-mattia-kat-walkthrough/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/>

- After Day Release, accept Lucia's call asking for a ride.
- Drive to the Key Lento Community Center before 4pm.
- Drive her home to finish.
> Tip: Turn up in a car, because Lucia complains if you arrive on foot.
> Tip: The call may only come once your relationship is high enough. If it doesn't come, carry on with the story.

### Clear Skies [Confirmed]
Who: Jason
- Go to the seaplane in Southwind in the afternoon, then talk to Brian at the Tiki bar.
- Take off from the water and fly to the intercept point to the south-east.
- Fly right above the other plane to hide it from radar until it lands at the swamp airstrip.
- Land at the northwest airstrip, fight off the bikers and rescue Donnie from the building.
- Cover Donnie until his plane takes off, then follow on a bike to the meeting point.
> Tip: Stay in cover and only pop out to shoot.
> Tip: If Donnie dies, you restart from the last checkpoint.
Sources: PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-clear-skies-join-donnie-on-a-job-walkthrough/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/>

### Swamped [Confirmed]
Who: Jason
- Go into the back room of Brian's Marina and ride with Brian to the Kingfisher camp in the northwest.
- Follow Brian on foot and inspect the pig pen.
- Fight your way out with Brian, then take the helicopter after the kidnappers' boat.
- Switch to a boat, pull right up beside the getaway boat and hold jump to board it, then win the button-mash fight.
- Back home, tell Lucia about Brian's retirement for a relationship boost.
> Tip: Move fast in the boat chase. Enemy fire can wreck your engine and send you back to a checkpoint.
! Missable: Reportedly, you can no longer sell to Donnie after this mission.

- After Feng Shui, accept Cal's text challenging you to a fishing contest.
- Take a boat to the marked spot in Gambit Bay and drop anchor from the vehicle menu.
- Equip the fishing rod and keep fishing until you land a stoplight parrotfish over 2 lbs.
- Send the catch to Cal to win.
> Tip: Cal's fishing pauses during the Tourist Trap run and comes back later.
> Tip: The basic Small Saltwater Lure is enough. The challenge stays open after Chapter 1, but the boat at Jason's house won't.

- Have a drink with Cal out back (optional).
- Choose Give Ride Home and help him out to the car.
- Drive him home carefully and help him up the stairs.

### Tourist Trap [Confirmed]
Who: Both
- As Jason, go to the Visitor Center in the far south of Key Lento and take the bag from the lockers.
- Go along with the arrest and questioning that follow.
- When the shooting starts, take over as Lucia and fight back.
- Get Jason to the docks and onto the boat while holding off the police.
- Head out to open water, then on to Cal's house.
> Tip: Killing police during the escape doesn't affect your Criminal Profile.
> Tip: Keep your pistol raised while you load Jason onto the boat.
! Missable: Point of no return. Make a manual save in a separate slot before starting.
! Missable: You permanently lose Jason's Key Lento house and all pocket cash. Put at least $5,000 into Family Savings first.
! Missable: You are locked into story missions with no free roam until Chapter 2. Finish Shirley's errands, Ring the Bell! and the balcony meditation before you start.

## Chapter 2 · Paradise Lost
Lucia carries the story alone at the Starlet Motel in Kelly County until Jason recovers. Coffee Break brings back character switching, and you need $15,000 to leave for Vice City.

### What's in the Bag? [Confirmed]
Who: Lucia
Unlocks: Paradise Lost trophy, Lucia in free roam
- As Lucia, look over the items from the Visitor Center bag in the motel room.
- Pick up the small statue and turn it over to read the note underneath.
- Check the hard drive. You have no computer yet, so it has to wait.
Sources: PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-whats-in-the-bag-walkthrough/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/>

### Take Care of Jason [Confirmed]
Who: Lucia
- Come back to the Starlet Motel at the end of each day to look in on Jason.
- Keep it up until a cutscene plays of Lucia caring for him.
- Carry on with the other Chapter 2 jobs. This one finishes by itself after Breakfast Run.
> Tip: It has no map marker of its own. Find it under Map > Story. You can't fail or miss it.
Sources: PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-take-care-of-jason-walkthrough/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/>

### Overnight Charges [Confirmed]
Who: Lucia
- Step out of the motel room and go into reception, on your left.
- Speak to Linda, the manager, who wants the room paid for now.
- If you bought the necklace in Chapter 1, Lucia gives it to Linda as collateral and the mission ends.
> Tip: You get the necklace back once you pay the $900 bill later (Behind on Payments).
> Tip: In reception, read the wall map to reveal Port Gellhorn, and the flyer to pick up Check Out McKittrick Farm.
Sources: PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-full-walkthrough-all-missions/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/>; PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-overnight-charges-see-linda-the-manager-walkthrough/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/overnight-charges>

### Just Moved In [Confirmed]
Who: Lucia
- Cross the road from the motel to the gas station and go into the Alpha convenience store.
- Grab the eight marked items: soda, protein bars, fruit, soup, gauze, bandanas, hair clippers and a makeup bag.
- Take them back to your motel room.
