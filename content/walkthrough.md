# GTA 6 Story Walkthrough

## Prologue
A short Lucia-only tutorial set in prison, with a flashback robbery. It teaches aiming, threatening, quick-time events and police searches.

### Prologue [Confirmed]
Who: Lucia
- Chat with your cellmates in Hamlet Prison (zoom with L2/LT, interact with X/A).
- Follow the guard to the counsellor's office.
- In the flashback, hold up the payday-loan shop and take the cash.
- Pass the quick-time event to shake off the bystander who grabs you.
- Run from the police and head back toward the coffee shop.
> Tip: You cannot lose the police here. The arrest near the coffee shop is scripted, so don't waste time trying to escape.
> Tip: The quick-time event is the only real way to fail.
Sources: PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-prologue-walkthrough/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/>

Jason settles into Key Lento: Ammu-Nation, his house and tenants, the BuckMe bank app and Lucia's release. Several errands are missable, and Tourist Trap is a major point of no return.

### Gear Up [Confirmed]
Who: Jason
Unlocks: A long gun of your choice, Ammu-Nation
- Walk into the Ammu-Nation near Jason's house in Key Lento.
- Talk to the clerk behind the counter.
- Buy a long gun: a shotgun or a tactical rifle.
- Step outside and fight off the three Kingfisher gang members who arrive in a truck.
- Also listed as Pick Up Another Gun in some guides.
> Tip: Early on you can probably only afford the shotgun.
> Tip: Win or die, the shootout ends the mission. Dying just respawns you at home, so there is no penalty.

### Buck Me [Confirmed]
Who: Jason
Unlocks: BuckMe app, Checking account with a $100 gift, Family Savings
- Go to the Fleeca bank in Key Lento and ask the teller about an account.
- Talk to the manager, who sends you to the BuckMe app instead.
- Open BuckMe on your phone.
- Move $100 from checking into Family Savings.
> Tip: Shops only take cash up to $999. Bigger purchases go through BuckMe.
> Tip: Dying costs pocket cash and some checking, so deposit cash at ATMs often (use the app's ATM Finder).
> Tip: Family Savings is locked for good, but it can never be lost. Savings goals give discounts and higher checking limits.
Sources: PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-buck-me-set-up-bank-account-walkthrough/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/>

### Tourist Trap [Confirmed]
Who: Both
- As Jason, go to the Visitor Center in the far south of Key Lento and take the bag from the lockers.
- Go along with the arrest and questioning that follow.
- When the shooting starts, take over as Lucia and fight back.
- Get Jason to the docks and onto the boat while holding off the police.
- Head out to open water, then on to Cal's house.
> Tip: Killing police during the escape doesn't affect your Criminal Profile.
> Tip: Keep your pistol raised while you load Jason onto the boat.
! Missable: Point of no return. Make a manual save in a separate slot before starting.
! Missable: You permanently lose Jason's Key Lento house and all pocket cash. Put at least $5,000 into Family Savings first.
! Missable: You are locked into story missions with no free roam until Chapter 2. Finish Shirley's errands, Ring the Bell! and the balcony meditation before you start.
Sources: PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-tourist-trap-take-up-mattias-offer-walkthrough/>; KeenGamer <https://www.keengamer.com/articles/guides/gta-6-walkthrough-all-chapters-and-story-missions-in-order/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/tourist-trap>

### Coffee Break [Confirmed]
Who: Both
Unlocks: Free switching between Jason and Lucia (R1/RB), Partner commands (hold D-Pad Right), Thrillbillies, Hit Avery Pawn, Hit Kingpin Bowling, Impounded Vehicle
- Walk with Lucia to the motel kitchen in the southern corner.
- Buy her a $2 coffee from the machine.
- Head back to the car.
- Hold D-Pad Right to give your partner a command.
> Tip: This is where switching between the two characters unlocks.
> Tip: The four side jobs all appear at once. Do them in any order; you need them all anyway, and they count toward the $15,000.
Sources: PowerPyx <https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-coffee-break-walkthrough/>; GTABase <https://www.gtabase.com/gta-6/missions/>

### Checking Out [Confirmed]
Who: Both
- After Cal calls, go to the motel room and use the bathroom door to start.
- Meet up with Lucia and fight off the attackers.
- As Lucia, follow Jason on a bike, then jump across to the car.
- Escape with Jason, shooting out the police cars' tyres.
> Tip: You need $15,000 and every Chapter 2 main mission done before this starts.
> Tip: Grab the shotgun on the bed. Don't crash the bike, or you get thrown off.
> Tip: If you die on the bike and Jason vanishes while the search stays active, restart the mission.
! Missable: Point of no return. This ends Chapter 2 and you lose the Starlet Motel as a home for good. Make a manual save first.

- About 25 minutes into The End of the Road, a police roadblock on a bridge brings up two prompts.
- Choose Swim for it (Triangle on PS5) or No Surrender (R2).
- Output Lag says Swim for it has two versions, decided before you press the button, and the trigger is unknown.
- After the credits, the Story Complete screen puts you back in free roam just before the finale, so you can replay it and pick the other prompt.
> Tip: Sources disagree on what else matters. Beebom and Dork say Criminal Profile tier, relationship level and Family Savings shape the ending. Output Lag's testing found no such check. Treat those factors as unverified.
